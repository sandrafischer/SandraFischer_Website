Corporate Identity ALUFEFA GmbH

A collaborative diploma thesis with ALUFEFA GmbH at HTL Braunau — developing a target-oriented social media strategy, on-site photography, an adaptive Canva template system, an operational handbook, and unified corporate print media.

Client ALUFEFA GmbH
Context HTL Diploma Thesis
Timeline 2023 – 2024
Deliverables Strategy, Photo, Print & Social

Social Media Strategy & Template System

Conducted as a two-person diploma thesis with Andrea Gaisbauer, this project established a structured brand identity and communication framework for ALUFEFA GmbH. Following extensive competitor and target-audience research, we redesigned the profile structure, highlight iconography, and editorial schedule.

To ensure sustainable continuity for the internal team, we engineered an intuitive post and reel-cover template system in Canva featuring dynamic color-matching banners aligned with the company's architectural aesthetic.

Content Production, Print & Handover

Across multiple production days at ALUFEFA's facility, we captured team portraits, manufacturing workflows, and specialized products (including ventilation grilles and eSafe parcel lockboxes) using a Sony Alpha 7 III with tailored Lightroom presets.

Alongside managing live postings over several weeks, the project delivered modern trifold image folders for ALUFEFA and its partner brands, updated corporate business cards with integrated vCard QR routing, and a comprehensive handover manual.
