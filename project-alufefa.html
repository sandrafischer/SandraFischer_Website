Corporate Identity & Social Media ALUFEFA

Diploma thesis in cooperation with ALUFEFA GmbH at HTL Braunau — developing a target-oriented social media strategy, on-site photography, a modular design template system, an operational handbook, and corporate stationery.

Client / Partner ALUFEFA GmbH
Timeline 2023 – 2024
My Focus Strategy, Photo, Templates, Cards
Context HTL Braunau Diploma Thesis

Social Media Strategy & Template System

To give the specialized technical company a distinct, reliable voice on Instagram and Facebook, I conducted competitive and target audience analyses and restructured the profile biography and highlight system. I engineered a flexible post and reel-cover template system in Canva with dynamic color-matching capabilities, ensuring high brand recognition across diverse factory and product imagery.

Content Production & Corporate Print

Across multiple production days at ALUFEFA's facility, I captured department portraits, product photography (ventilation grilles, eSafe units), and active manufacturing imagery using a Sony Alpha 7 III with Lightroom color grading. Alongside managing live scheduled postings, I authored a comprehensive step-by-step Social Media Handbook for internal company handoff and redesigned the official two-sided corporate business card with integrated digital vCard QR routing.
