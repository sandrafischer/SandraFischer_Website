Personal Brand & Motion Kit

A cohesive personal identity system developed at FH Hagenberg — uniting an expressive monogram based on the initials FS, refined typography, print collateral, and a dynamic broadcast motion design kit.

Context FH Hagenberg (MTD)
Discipline Brand Identity & Motion
Timeline 2026
Deliverables Monogram, Print & Motion Kit

The Monogram & Brand System

Created as an identity coursework project at FH Hagenberg, the design captures my approach at the intersection of media technology and graphic precision. The core mark merges the initials FS into a fluid, continuous loop that balances geometric clarity with organic movement.

The visual system operates across high-contrast dark tones, warm neutral paper backgrounds, and eucalyptus accents, establishing a confident presence across business cards, digital portfolios, and editorial documents.

Broadcast Motion Toolkit

To extend the brand into time-based media, the static marks were translated into kinetic elements in Adobe After Effects. The motion kit establishes a consistent visual language for video presentations, showreels, and multimedia projects.

The kit includes five modular animation assets: an organic logo reveal, minimalist lower thirds for speaker identification, animated content inserts, kinetic dividers, and a full intro sequence.
