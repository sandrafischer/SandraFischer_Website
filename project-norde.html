Norde Digital Platform

A digital flagship experience combining brutalist layout principles with ultra-smooth interaction design and accessible web performance.

Client Norde Studio
Year 2025
Disciplines UI/UX Design, Web Dev, GSAP
Live Link Visit Website ↗

The Challenge

Norde Studio needed a digital platform that authentically reflects their physical craftsmanship while providing an intuitive, interactive experience for global clients. The goal was to break free from traditional template aesthetics and introduce tactile micro-interactions.

The Solution

We established a bespoke design system rooted in Swiss typography, fine paper tones, and fluid interactive choreography. Using semantic HTML, lightweight CSS architectures, and performant JavaScript animations, the platform achieves both artistic distinction and top-tier loading speeds.
