Brand System Feichtinger Ortungstechnik

A holistic corporate identity for an independent locating specialist — uniting mark precision, tactile stationery, technical flyers, and a comprehensive brand guide.

Client Feichtinger Ortungstechnik
Year 2026
Scope Logo, Stationery, Brandbook, Print
Discipline Corporate Identity & Print

The Brand Foundation

The visual identity translates the core principles of non-destructive locating: precision, acoustic focus, and technological certainty. The mark pairs a distinctive geometric symbol (visualizing pulse frequencies and structural localization) with a solid, highly legible wordmark designed to stand out on both digital assets and high-visibility workwear.

Print Collateral & Brandbook

From premium business cards with tactile finishing to structured corporate stationery, service flyers, and official invoices, every touchpoint reinforces reliability. A detailed Brandbook defines typography scales, strict safety clearance ratios, and color recipes across CMYK, RGB, and vehicle wrap production.
