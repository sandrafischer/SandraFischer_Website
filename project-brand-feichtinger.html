Brand System Feichtinger Ortungstechnik

A full corporate brand identity for an independent leak detection practice — uniting custom mark geometry, vibrant turquoise and purple color coding, tactile stationery, large-scale construction site banners, and brand guidelines.

Client Ortungstechnik Feichtinger
Discipline Corporate Identity & Print
Year 2026
Deliverables Logo, Print Collateral & Brandbook

The Waterdrop & Lettermark Concept

The core emblem visualizes the precision of non-destructive acoustic and thermographic locating. The design seamlessly merges the organic silhouette of a waterdrop with the initial letter A, representing both Feichtinger and advanced measurement technology (Messtechnik).

Diverging from conventional trade aesthetics, the system utilizes a vibrant combination of technical turquoise and rich violet. This color palette ensures instant high-contrast recognition across digital interfaces, print collateral, and field equipment.

Comprehensive Print Suite & Brandbook

The physical brand experience spans premium double-sided business cards, corporate letterheads, detailed service flyers, and heavy-duty construction site fence banners (Bauzaunbanner) engineered for maximum outdoor visibility.

The identity is documented in an extensive Brandbook, detailing typography hierarchies, color values across CMYK, RGB, and vehicle vinyl reproduction, alongside rules for utilizing the custom illustrated locator mascot.
