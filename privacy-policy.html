2. Web Hosting & Server Log Files

When accessing this website, the hosting provider automatically collects and stores technical information in server log files that your browser transmits automatically. This includes:

  • Browser type and browser version
  • Operating system used
  • Referrer URL (previously visited page)
  • Host name of the accessing computer
  • Date and time of the server request
  • IP address (anonymized where applicable)

This data is processed to ensure system stability, security, and technical error diagnosis. The legal basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of our website pursuant to Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR. Server log data is automatically deleted after a maximum period of 14 to 30 days unless required for security incidents.

3. External Libraries & Content Delivery Networks (CDN)

This site loads external scripts to enhance user experience and smooth scrolling (e.g., Lenis via unpkg.com, operated by Cloudflare, Inc.). When these assets are loaded, your IP address and technical device data are transmitted to the server of the CDN provider to deliver the file. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an aesthetically pleasing and performant presentation of our portfolio pursuant to Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR.

4. Contact Form & Inquiries (Web3Forms)

If you contact us via email or the integrated contact form, your provided information (name, email address, project requirements, and message content) will be collected and processed to handle your inquiry and for potential follow-up questions.

Our contact form uses the service Web3Forms. When you submit the form, your submitted data is securely transmitted via SSL encryption to Web3Forms servers to route the email to us. Web3Forms acts as a data processor according to Art. 28 GDPR.

The legal basis for processing this data is the performance of pre-contractual measures or fulfilling a contract pursuant to Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR, or our legitimate interest in answering your message pursuant to Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR. Your inquiry data remains stored until the purpose for processing ceases to apply (e.g., after the project is completed) or you request its deletion, provided there are no statutory retention obligations.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your stored personal data at any time:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure ("Right to be forgotten", Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21 GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at hello@sandrafischer.at.
