Privacy Policy

General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) compliance statement regarding data collection, contact processing, and website security.

1. General Overview & Controller

The responsible controller for data processing on this website is Sandra Fischer (Mettmach, Upper Austria, hello@sandrafischer.at). The protection of your personal data is handled with the highest priority and strictly in accordance with GDPR regulations.

2. Web Hosting & Server Logs

When visiting this website, technical server log files (such as IP address, browser type, operating system, and time of access) are automatically collected for technical stability and security purposes. This data is not merged with other personal data sources.

3. Contact Form & Inquiries

When you submit a project inquiry via our contact form (powered by Web3Forms), the information you provide (name, email address, project type, and message) is securely processed to answer your request. This data is not shared with unauthorized third parties.

4. Your Rights

You have the right to request information about your stored personal data at any time, as well as the right to demand its correction, blocking, or deletion. To exercise these rights, simply reach out via email to hello@sandrafischer.at.
