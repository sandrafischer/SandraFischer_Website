COLLABORATION & FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working together, timelines, and deliverables.

I specialize in comprehensive brand identities, responsive web design & frontend development, 3D spatial visualizations, and editorial photography. Whether you need a full rebrand or a specialized digital touchpoint, I handle both end-to-end projects and standalone creative deliverables.

Every project begins with a discovery phase to define strategy, scope, and aesthetic direction. From there, we move into iterative design cycles with structured feedback checkpoints. For web projects, I build and test responsive production code; for brand/print, you receive press-ready files and asset kits.

Timelines depend on project complexity. A focused branding or landing page sprint takes approximately 2–3 weeks, while comprehensive web builds or multi-asset identity systems generally run between 4–6 weeks. Fast-track options are available upon request.

Both! I am based in Upper Austria and work with clients worldwide via asynchronous communication, Figma reviews, and video calls. For photography shoots and initial strategic workshops, on-site collaboration is always possible.
