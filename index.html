ABOUT

Sandra Fischer

I study Media Technology & Design at FH Hagenberg — one of Austria's most technically rigorous design programs — and work as an independent designer from Mettmach, Upper Austria.

My practice sits at the intersection of technical craft and visual culture. I take projects from initial concept through to final implementation — whether that's a pixel-sharp web build, a press-ready corporate identity, or a photographic campaign.
