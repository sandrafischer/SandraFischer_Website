ABOUT

Sandra Fischer

I study Media Technology & Design at FH Hagenberg and work as an independent designer from Mettmach, Upper Austria.

My practice sits at the intersection of technical craft and graphic elegance. I take projects from initial concept through to final delivery — whether that's a responsive web build, a comprehensive brand identity, or a photographic campaign.

FAQ

Both full projects and smaller tasks. Whether it’s a complete website, a brand identity, motion animations, a 3D visual, a photo session, or a single print flyer.

Just send me a message. You don’t need a finished concept or a long document to begin with, a rough idea or a quick question is more than enough. We talk about what you need, I share design previews along the way, and we adjust things until you are happy with the final result.

Alongside my studies, I have around 10-20 hours a week for client work. Smaller tasks are usually done within a few days, while full websites or brandings take about 2 to 4 weeks. If you’re in a rush, just let me know and we can speed things up.

Every project is different, so I don’t have fixed package prices. After a first chat, I’ll give you a clear and fair quote tailored to what you need. We will definitely find a solution and a budget that works well for both of us.

Yes, absolutely. A brand or website is meant to grow. I’m happy to support you post-launch with technical website maintenance, updates, new print designs, or custom social media templates whenever you need them.
