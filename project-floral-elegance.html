Floral Elegance Editorial Series

A soft-light fashion and portrait editorial created during the HTL Braunau media workshop — exploring organic natural textures, tactile denim details, and summer minimalism.

Context HTL Media Workshop
Timeline Media Week
Discipline Editorial & Fashion Portrait
Lighting Setup High-Key Studio Diffuser

Concept & Tactile Textures

Floral Elegance focuses on the delicate interplay between raw textile surfaces and fragile botanical elements. By placing freshly picked wild field daisies directly into washed denim pockets, the shoot creates a tactile contrast between structured casual fashion and pure organic lightness.

Soft Light Studio Direction

The session utilized clean high-key studio diffusion against seamless neutral grey-to-white gradients. Careful reflector positioning eliminated harsh shadows while preserving the natural skin tones, fine fabric weaves, and delicate petal structures of the flower bouquets.
