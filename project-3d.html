The Alpine Sanctuary & 3D Explorations

A high-immersion architectural wellness retreat modeled and rendered entirely in Blender — exploring atmospheric lighting contrasts, material tactile physics, and asset development.

Context FH Hagenberg (3D Design)
Timeline WS 2025/26
Tool Blender (Cycles Render)
Focus Lighting, Materials & Sculpting

The Concept: Warmth vs. Winter

The Alpine Sanctuary was developed as the comprehensive final term project in 3D Design[cite: 15]. The spatial concept creates tension between the freezing mountain outdoors and a safe, protective sanctuary within[cite: 15]. Anchored around a custom marble hot tub situated by a floor-to-ceiling panoramic window, the scene balances rustic stone masonry, heavy timber ceiling beams, an open wood fireplace, and an integrated rain shower[cite: 15].

Materiality, Sculpting & Lighting

From initial floor plan sketches to final Cycles rendering, each asset was custom built[cite: 15]. Pillar candles were crafted with organic wax melting profiles via digital sculpting[cite: 15], silver wall lanterns cast warm ambient accents[cite: 15], and stone texture displacement maps provide physical tactile depth[cite: 15]. Atmospheric lighting utilizes volumetric warmth from the fireplace and glowing candle wicks against the cool winter exterior[cite: 15].
