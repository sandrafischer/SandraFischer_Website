Good Eats Organic Restaurant Identity

An academic concept identity created at HTL Braunau — developing a modern brand system for a healthy food franchise, uniting custom mark typography, organic color coding, corporate stationery, and apparel mockups.

Context HTL Braunau
Discipline Brand Identity & Print
Year 2023
Deliverables Logo, Stationery & Merch

The Brand Concept & Mark

Developed as a comprehensive coursework assignment in media design, Good Eats represents a modern dining franchise focused on organic, plant-based nutrition. The visual mark combines a chef’s hat, cutlery, and three sprouting leaves into an iconic silhouette that doubles as a growing tree to symbolize natural vitality.

Anchored by the claim „Where Flavor and Health Unite“, the system pairs natural forest and leaf greens with neutral stone tones and crisp yellow accents to project warmth, cleanliness, and culinary quality.

Typography, Print & Merchandise

The geometric sans-serif typeface family Barlow (specifically Barlow Semi Condensed) was chosen for its clean, contemporary rhythm and high legibility across menus, packaging, and digital signage.

The identity extends across a full corporate collateral suite, including double-sided business cards, letterheads, branded envelopes, seasonal social media promotional campaign layouts, and embroidered merchandise concepts for caps and hoodies.
