Good Eats Organic Restaurant Identity

A fresh, sustainable corporate design system for a healthy food franchise — combining clean modern typography, custom icon marks, tactile stationery, and branded merchandise.

Context HTL Braunau (MDS)
Timeline 2022 – 2023
Claim Where Flavor & Health Unite
Scope Logo, Stationery, Social, Merch

The Brand Concept & Mark

Good Eats focuses on organic, plant-based nutrition and sustainable quality. The mark merges a chef's hat, culinary cutlery, and three sprouting leaves into an iconic silhouette that doubles as a growing tree. Paired with the sans-serif Barlow typeface family and an earthy palette of natural greens, clean whites, and fresh accents, the design radiates warmth and biological trust.

Stationery, Social & Merchandise

The visual system scales across every restaurant touchpoint: double-sided business cards with a generous watermark emblem, structured business stationery (envelopes, letterheads, email signatures), promotional campaign flyers, social media templates for World Vegan Month, and apparel including snapback caps and hoodies.

01. Mark & Typography

Tree-sprout chef emblem & Barlow type

02. Business Stationery

03. Social Campaigns

Seasonal promo posts & flyer layout

04. Merchandising

Screen-printed hoodies & caps
