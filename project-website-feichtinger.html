Website Feichtinger Ortungstechnik

A digital presence for an independent leak detection & locating specialist — combining rugged industrial reliability with high-precision UI engineering and interactive motion design.

Client Feichtinger Ortungstechnik
Year 2026
Disciplines UI/UX Design, Web Dev, Motion
Live Link Live Prototype ↗

The Challenge

Launching an independent locating and leak-detection practice requires instant credibility and frictionless emergency routing. The digital experience had to serve two distinct user groups: stressed homeowners in need of urgent water damage help and B2B professionals such as insurance assessors and plumbing firms requiring technical rigor.

The Solution

We established a confident layout structured around clear service hierarchies, tactile emergency CTAs, and a technical aesthetic that reflects the precision of acoustic and thermographic locating. Custom motion choreographies illustrate the non-destructive measurement techniques before drilling begins.
