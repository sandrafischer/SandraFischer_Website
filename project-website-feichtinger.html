Website Ortungstechnik Feichtinger

A modern, high-performance web experience for an independent leak detection specialist — featuring instant emergency calling, automated appointment booking, clear service hierarchies, and an interactive mascot.

Client Ortungstechnik Feichtinger
Discipline Web Design & Development
Year 2026
Deliverables Framer Prototype & Web App ↗

Emergency Routing & Clear Service Structure

In the event of acute water damage or pipe bursts, rapid assistance is critical. The interface prioritizes a prominent emergency call trigger that connects stressed property owners directly with the specialist with a single tap.

The core structure clearly presents non-destructive locating methods, distinct benefits, and service areas, establishing immediate trust for both private homeowners and B2B partners such as plumbers and insurance assessors.

Interactive Mascot & Direct Appointment Booking

Alongside a structured inquiry form, the website integrates a self-service appointment scheduler, enabling clients to book non-emergency diagnostic sessions smoothly online.

To give the technical trade a warm, approachable character, the custom locator mascot was animated as lightweight Lottie micro-interactions throughout the responsive layout, guiding users dynamically toward key actions.
